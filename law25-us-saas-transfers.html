Quebec Law 25 Section 17: Transferring Personal Information to US SaaS Providers

Key fact for Ontario, BC, Alberta & Quebec SMBs: Quebec's Law 25 has no minimum revenue threshold and applies whenever your organization collects personal information from even one Quebec resident.

Why US SaaS Tools Trigger a Mandatory Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA / ÉFVP)

Under Section 17 of Quebec's private-sector privacy act (as amended by Law 25), before communicating personal information outside Quebec — including hosting customer emails, invoices, or analytics on US cloud servers — your business must conduct a Privacy Impact Assessment evaluating:

  • The sensitivity of the personal information transferred.
  • The purposes for which the information will be used.
  • The protection measures (encryption, Data Processing Addenda, access controls) in place.
  • The legal framework applicable in the destination jurisdiction (e.g., United States).

Common SaaS Tools Requiring Section 17 Documentation

Google Workspace, Stripe, HubSpot, Mailchimp, Shopify, QuickBooks Online, Slack, Google Analytics 4, Meta Pixel, and OpenAI API all commonly process or store data on US infrastructure by default.
